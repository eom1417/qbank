QBank sample bank: general medicine

1. Which ECG change is the earliest typical sign of hyperkalemia?

A. Peaked T waves

B. U waves

C. Delta wave

D. ST depression

Answer: A

Explanation: Rising potassium first makes T waves tall and narrow; U waves suggest hypokalemia.

2. What is the first-line treatment of anaphylaxis?

A. Oral antihistamine

B. Intramuscular adrenaline

C. Intravenous hydrocortisone

D. Nebulised salbutamol

Answer: B

Explanation: Intramuscular adrenaline treats airway swelling and shock; the others are adjuncts.

3. Which vitamin deficiency causes scurvy?

A. Vitamin A

B. Vitamin B12

C. Vitamin C

D. Vitamin D

Answer: C

Explanation: Vitamin C is needed to make collagen; without it gums bleed and wounds heal poorly.

4. Which organism most commonly causes bronchiolitis in infants?

A. Adenovirus

B. Respiratory syncytial virus

C. Influenza virus

D. Streptococcus pneumoniae

Answer: B

Explanation: RSV causes most cases of bronchiolitis, mainly in winter and in children under two.

5. Which heart valve is most often affected by rheumatic heart disease?

A. Aortic valve

B. Mitral valve

C. Tricuspid valve

D. Pulmonary valve

Answer: B

Explanation: The mitral valve is involved in most cases, often as mitral stenosis years later.

6. Which hormone does the posterior pituitary release to concentrate urine?

A. Aldosterone

B. Antidiuretic hormone

C. Renin

D. Cortisol

Answer: B

Explanation: ADH (vasopressin) makes the collecting ducts reabsorb water.

7. Which antibody class crosses the placenta?

A. IgA

B. IgE

C. IgG

D. IgM

Answer: C

Explanation: Only IgG crosses the placenta, which gives the newborn passive immunity.

8. Which electrolyte disturbance is the classic cause of tetany?

A. Hypocalcemia

B. Hypercalcemia

C. Hypernatremia

D. Hyperkalemia

Answer: A

Explanation: Low calcium makes nerves excitable: tingling, cramps and carpopedal spasm.

9. Which vaccine is commonly given at birth in many countries?

A. BCG

B. MMR

C. Varicella

D. HPV

Answer: A

Explanation: BCG and the first dose of hepatitis B are given at birth in many schedules.

10. Which is the most common type of anemia worldwide?

A. Iron deficiency anemia

B. Vitamin B12 deficiency anemia

C. Sickle cell anemia

D. Aplastic anemia

Answer: A

Explanation: Iron deficiency is the leading cause of anemia across all age groups worldwide.
