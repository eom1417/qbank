# ADR-[NNN]: [decision in one line]

Copy to `docs/adr/NNN-short-title.md` for irreversible or high-impact choices only (data store,
hosting, authentication, payments, public contracts). Link it from `BLUEPRINT.md` §9.

- **Date / status:** [YYYY-MM-DD] · proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-[NNN]
- **Context:** the forces at play, in 3-5 sentences, with the constraint that decides it.
- **Decision:** what we do.
- **Alternatives:** each rejected option and the one reason it lost.
- **Consequences:** what gets easier, what gets harder, what we must now maintain.
- **Reversal trigger:** the measurable signal that would make us revisit this.
